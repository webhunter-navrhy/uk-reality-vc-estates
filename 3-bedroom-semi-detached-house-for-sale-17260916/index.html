About the property

Description property

This beautifully presented and thoughtfully decorated three bed semi-detached property is situated in the sought after location of Harestock; finished to an excellent standard throughout.

Ground Floor:

The ground floor begins with a welcoming and beautifully decorated hallway. There is a downstairs cloakroom and large under stairs storage cupboard.

The modern Kitchen, situated at the front of the house, is fitted with taupe units and integrated appliances: oven and hob, microwave, dishwasher and fridge/freezer and additional freezer.

To the rear the 16ft L shaped living room features a chimney breast with oak mantle. The conservatory which is filled with light, currently used as a dining room and additional entertaining space, is a welcomed addition and has French doors to the patio/garden.

First Floor:

Upstairs is a light landing with airing cupboard. There are three well-proportioned bedrooms, two doubles and a single. The master bedroom and bedroom two benefit from a built in wardrobe cupboard. There is a modern family bathroom complete with shower over bath, basin and toilet unit and vanity cupboard storage.

Externally:

The property benefits from a sunny landscaped, low maintenance garden with lawn and patio area. There is side access via a gate. The property has a garage in the block and private residents parking is available with the potential to add a driveway, subject to planning, if desired. The roof was completely replaced in 2026.

This home is well maintained, thoughtfully modernised and decorated in a well connected location. Early viewing is highly recommend.
